# Scope

> Source: Spec — "Scope".

Moderation is set up for four content areas — the four kinds of place a learner can write.

| Content area | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Product discussions** | The discussion under any course, bundle, batch, mock test, test series, ebook, webinar, free resource, podcast or custom product |
| **Community** | Posts and comments |
| **Feeds** | Posts and comments |
| **Newsfeed** | Discussions |

Four rows, however many products the academy has. Adding a course does not add a row — its discussion is
already covered by Product discussions.

A single product that needs to differ is an **exception**. Created in Moderation → Settings, listed there,
removable. Most academies will have none.

Each area is switched on or off by the academy. Comments are checked like the posts they sit under.

## What is checked, by version

**v1** text and pictures · **v2** direct messages, live chat, reviews, doubts, profiles · **later** video and audio.

## Who is never checked

Teachers and Teaching Assistants are never checked.
