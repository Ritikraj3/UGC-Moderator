# Two settings, two different questions

> Source: Spec — "Two settings, two different questions".

## Content review mode — does content appear before or after the check?

| | Review first | Publish first |
|---|---|---|
| **The moment they post** | Held, marked waiting for review | Live |
| **Clean** | Published a second later | Nothing happens |
| **Breaks a rule** | The rule's action applies. Nobody else saw it | The rule's action applies. It comes down |
| **Suits** | Under-18 academies, free signup, small or new communities | Busy communities |

**Default: review first.**

## Moderation mode — who acts on a flag once it is raised?

| Mode | AI runs? | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Autopilot** | Yes | The rule's action applies on its own |
| **Copilot** | Yes | Nothing is stopped automatically — everything flagged goes to the queue |
| **AI disabled** | No | Blocked words and learner reports only |

## Where they are set

Both are set for the academy, then per content area, and an exception can differ from its area.

A rule can be pinned to Copilot even on Autopilot. **Rule 5, self-harm, is pinned by default.**
