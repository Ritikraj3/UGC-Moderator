# What the learner sees

> Source: Spec — "What the learner sees".

Never machine-written text — the learner sees the academy's own rule.

> **Can't be published — Rule 6: No spam**
>
> No promotions, advertising or selling of any kind. Edit your post and try again.

## By situation

| Situation | What the learner sees |
|---|---|
| **Blocked** | Post never created. Nothing to edit or restore — they're still in the composer |
| **Held** | Post exists, visible only to them, marked **Waiting for review**. Worded as a check, not a verdict, because held posts are often approved |
| **Taken down after publishing** | Under publish first, their post goes up and then comes down. They are told the rule |
| **Self-harm** | Never names the rule. Fixed message plus a helpline |
| **After a decision** | Always told — live, or the rule and why. A held post that goes silent reads as ignored |

## Editing a held post

Editing a held post re-runs the checks. Clean now, it publishes. Still breaking, it goes to the back of the queue
flagged **edited after being held**.

Every version is kept, and the Teacher sees the history — otherwise this works: post abuse, get held, edit to
something innocent, get approved, edit back.

## A blocked post still leaves a record

A blocked post still leaves a record — **text, rule, author, time**. Not a post, never restorable, but without it
there's no evidence in a dispute, nothing counting toward a restriction, and nothing feeding the reports.
