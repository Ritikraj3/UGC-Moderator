# The review queue · Members who keep breaking rules

> Source: Spec — "The review queue" and "Members who keep breaking rules".

## The review queue

The queue is the landing screen of Moderation, not something buried inside it.

**Three panes:** the list, the content in its thread, and who wrote it.

**Four tabs** — Needs review · Reported · Unchecked · Done. Every row shows the content and the reason, with
**Approve** and **Remove** on the row. Filter by content area, product, rule and date.

**The member panel** — joined, courses, posts written, past actions, current restriction. Most decisions are
about the person, not the post.

Restricting and banning happen here, beside the content that caused them. The **Restrictions & bans** screen is
the list and the undo.

Everything is undoable. Removed content is kept **90 days**.

**The header shows how long the oldest item has waited.** Indian IT rules give whoever hosts content 36 hours to
take down unlawful material — on a whitelabel platform that's each academy. That number is the clock.

## Members who keep breaking rules

Counted on confirmed removals, never flags.

Past the threshold their posts are all held — **the only automatic step**. Banning is always a person's decision,
and it does not cancel their course. The queue prompts: *"5 posts removed — ban this member?"*

A ban is either from **one product** or from **the whole academy**. An academy ban replaces any product ban that
learner already has.
